Research Persona & Background

Engineering Advanced Alloys at the Atomic & Continuum Scale

I am a Doctoral Scholar at Freiburg University, alumnus of IIT Madras (M.Tech), and former DAAD KOSPIE Fellow at TU Darmstadt, with foundation from RGUKT Nuzvid (B.Tech).

My research bridges physical metallurgy, thermodynamic simulations (CALPHAD), and modern machine learning frameworks. By combining advanced characterization (SEM, TEM, EBSD, Atom Probe Tomography) with predictive materials informatics, I design resilient, high-performance alloys tailored for aerospace and structural applications.
